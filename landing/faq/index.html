FAQ: swap without BNB and Gas Assist

Short answers about the self-custodial wallet, BEP-20 swaps with no BNB, and Gas Assist on BNB Chain.

Can I swap BEP-20 tokens without BNB?

Yes, when Gas Assist is available for that token and route. The gas is covered for you, and the cost is taken from the swap. It is not free: the fee is shown before you sign.

What is Gas Assist?

Gas Assist is a BNB Chain flow for wallets that hold BEP-20 tokens but no BNB to pay gas. The backend prepares an exact fee, approval, and swap package. You review the cost, then sign.

How Gas Assist works explains the steps in more detail.

Is Pistachio Swap a self-custodial wallet?

Yes. Your tokens stay in your own wallet. Nothing moves until you sign a transaction you have seen in full.

Does Pistachio Swap hold my funds?

No. Your tokens stay in your own wallet. Nothing moves until you sign a transaction you have seen in full.

Is Gas Assist free?

No. Network cost, sponsorship cost, and Pistachio Swap fees are shown in the quote before you sign.

Which networks are supported?

25 EVM networks, including Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Base and Avalanche. Gas Assist runs on BNB Smart Chain only.

Do I need a special wallet?

No. Any standard EVM wallet works. Pistachio Wallet is an optional passkey-protected wallet that lives in your own browser.

Is Pistachio Swap audited?

Not yet. It is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Use a separate wallet and small amounts.

Before you start

Pistachio Swap is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Swapping tokens carries risk, including permanent loss. Use a separate wallet and small amounts.
