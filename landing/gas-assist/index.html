How Gas Assist works on BNB Chain

Gas Assist lets you swap BEP-20 tokens without holding BNB for gas. It is part of the Pistachio Swap self-custodial wallet. It is not free, and it is not available for every token or route.

Open the wallet FAQ

The problem it solves

A wallet can hold valuable BEP-20 tokens and still be stuck, because sending or swapping on BNB Smart Chain needs BNB for gas. Buying BNB first is often the reason someone is stuck.

Gas Assist is built for that case. If the token and route are eligible, Pistachio Swap covers the gas. The cost is taken from the swap itself, and you see that cost before you sign.

What you sign

For an eligible prepaid Gas Assist swap, the backend prepares an exact three-transaction package:

  1. the fee-payment transfer
  2. the exact token approval
  3. the exact swap transaction

With Pistachio Wallet those three transactions are reviewed as one package and authorized through a single package-signing request. The wallet validates the package, requires a fresh passkey check for an already-unlocked session, signs each exact transaction locally, and only then returns the complete package. A partial package is not submitted.

If you connect a standard EVM wallet instead, you still review the quote first. The app does not hide the fee, the spender, or the minimum you receive.

What it is not

  • It is not a free swap. Network cost, sponsorship cost, and Pistachio Swap fees are disclosed in the quote.
  • It is not available on every network. Gas Assist runs on BNB Smart Chain only.
  • It is not available for every token or every route. When it is not, the app says so.
  • It is not custody. Tokens stay in your wallet until you sign.

If something changes

Quotes expire. If the fee, the approval, or the swap no longer matches what you reviewed, the package is abandoned. You are not asked to sign a different transaction than the one you saw.

Pistachio Swap is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Use a separate wallet and small amounts.
