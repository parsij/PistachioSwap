Gas assisted swaps on BNB Chain

Holding tokens you cannot move, because paying gas needs BNB you do not have? That is what Gas Assist is for.

  1. We prepare the whole swap The fee, the approval and the swap itself, as one exact package.
  2. You see the full cost first The exact fee and the minimum you receive, before anything is signed.
  3. One approval covers it With Pistachio Wallet the package is authorized once, then signed on your device.
  4. All or nothing If anything changes mid-flow, the whole package is abandoned.

Gas Assist is not free

The network cost, the sponsorship cost and our fee are all shown in the quote before you sign.

It is not available for every token or every route. When it is not, the app says so instead of failing quietly.

A self-custodial wallet, not a custodian

Your tokens stay in your wallet. Nothing moves until you sign a transaction you have seen in full.

You see the whole transaction

Where it goes, who can spend what, and how much — before you approve. What gets broadcast is checked against what you saw.

Routes compared after fees

Several exchanges and bridges are quoted, then ranked on what you actually receive — not the headline number.

Fake tokens stay obvious

A token that copies a real name with lookalike letters is never shown as the real thing. Unverified holdings are kept separate.

25 EVM networks

Gas assisted swaps run on BNB Smart Chain. Other networks use a normal swap when you hold gas.

Routed through Across, deBridge, Relay, Chainflip, 0x, KyberSwap and Uniswap.

Your wallet, or the one in your browser

Any standard EVM wallet works. Pistachio Wallet is optional, and lives on your device.

Keys never leave your device

Your key is held in an isolated worker and wiped on lock. The stored vault is encrypted and unlocked by your passkey.

A passkey check every time

Signing, revealing a recovery phrase or exporting a key each need a fresh check — not one unlock for the whole session.

You can always take it with you

Export a recovery phrase or an encrypted backup whenever you like. Removing it here does not touch anything on-chain.

Common questions

Short answers below. See the full FAQ for more detail.

Can I swap BEP-20 tokens without BNB?

Yes, when Gas Assist is available for that token and route. The gas is covered for you, and the cost is taken from the swap. It is not free: the fee is shown before you sign.

Is Pistachio Swap a self-custodial wallet?

Yes. Your tokens stay in your own wallet. Nothing moves until you sign a transaction you have seen in full.

Does Gas Assist work on every network?

No. Gas Assist runs on BNB Smart Chain. You can still swap on the other supported EVM networks when you hold that network’s gas token.

Before you start

Pistachio Swap is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Swapping tokens carries risk, including permanent loss. Use a separate wallet and small amounts.
