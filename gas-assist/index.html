Gas Assist: swap tokens when your wallet has no BNB for gas

A wallet can hold valuable BEP-20 tokens and still be stuck, because paying network gas needs BNB it does not have. Gas Assist sponsors that BNB so the swap can go through — and shows you the exact cost first.

The problem Gas Assist solves

Every on-chain action costs gas, and on BNB Chain gas is paid in BNB. If your wallet holds only BEP-20 tokens and no BNB, a normal swap simply cannot be sent — the wallet has nothing to pay the network with. Your tokens are not lost, but they are frozen in place.

Without Gas Assist

You would first have to get BNB into the wallet from somewhere else, just to unlock tokens you already hold.

With Gas Assist

Pistachio Swap fronts the BNB for the approval and the swap, and recovers it from the trade itself. No separate top-up.

You stay in control

Nothing is sponsored silently. The exact fee and the minimum you receive are shown before a single signature.

How a Gas Assist swap works

An eligible prepaid swap is prepared as one exact package of three transactions, authorized together.

  1. The package is prepared The backend builds three exact transactions: the fee payment, the token approval, and the swap.
  2. You review it once All three are shown together, with the exact fee and the minimum output, before any signature is requested.
  3. One authorization signs the package With Pistachio Wallet the package needs one review and one fresh passkey check. Each transaction is then signed on your device and the signed result validated.
  4. All or nothing A changed account, nonce, chain, expiry, or rewritten transaction aborts the whole package. A partial package is never submitted.

Why one package

The fee, the approval and the swap only make sense together. Binding them into a single reviewed, single-authorized package means you approve exactly what runs — not three separate prompts you have to reason about one at a time.

A fresh Gas Assist session may still ask for one message signature first. That signature authenticates your wallet; it does not authorize a transaction.

What Gas Assist costs

Gas Assist is not free, and it never pretends to be.

Three parts, all disclosed first

The quote or the review shows the network cost, the sponsorship cost, and the PistachioSwap fee before you sign. A gas-assisted swap therefore costs more than an ordinary swap where you pay gas yourself, because it also covers the sponsored BNB.

You see the minimum you receive

Alongside the fee, the review shows the minimum output for the trade, so the total effect on what you walk away with is on screen before you commit.

When Gas Assist is available

It is offered when the conditions for a safe sponsored swap are met, and withheld — visibly — when they are not.

Token eligibility

The token and route have to be supported and safely priceable.

Provider & liquidity

A provider has to offer the route with enough liquidity to fill it.

Policy & configuration

Sponsorship policy limits and backend configuration decide the rest. When a route is not eligible, the app tells you instead of failing quietly.

Gas Assist FAQ

What is Gas Assist?

Gas Assist lets a wallet that holds BEP-20 tokens but no BNB still swap on BNB Chain. Pistachio Swap sponsors the BNB gas needed for the approval and the swap, and the cost is taken from the trade instead of from a BNB balance you do not have.

Is Gas Assist free?

No. Gas Assist is not described as free. The network cost, the sponsorship cost, and PistachioSwap fees are disclosed by the quote or the review flow before you sign anything.

Which network does Gas Assist run on?

Gas Assist runs on BNB Smart Chain, for BEP-20 tokens. Pistachio Swap itself supports 25 EVM networks for ordinary swaps.

Is Gas Assist always available?

No. Availability depends on token eligibility, provider support, liquidity, policy limits, and backend configuration. When a route is not eligible, the app says so instead of failing quietly.

Can Gas Assist submit only part of my swap?

No. The three transactions are authorized as one package. Each signed transaction is validated, and any mismatch — a changed account, nonce, chain, expiry, or rewritten transaction — aborts the whole package. A partial package is never submitted.

Before you use it

Pistachio Swap is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Swapping tokens carries risk, including permanent loss. Use a separate wallet and small amounts while development continues.
